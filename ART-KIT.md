# AI GUILD IZUMO — Art Kit

## Immutable identity
**AI GUILD**  
**IZUMO**  
**IMAGINE / BUILD / SHARE**

Do not add explanatory copy beneath the motto.

## Direction
Contemporary Japanese minimalism × Izumo × technology. The identity should feel like a serious local creative/technical guild, not tourism, SaaS, anime, or generic cyberpunk AI.

### Visual vocabulary
- Warm off-white / uncoated paper
- Near-black typography
- Vermilion as the single dominant accent
- Large negative space
- One oversized red sun/circle
- Reduced Izumo landscape geometry
- Abstract Izumo Taisha / shimenawa / roofline
- Optional Kirara / Sea of Japan horizon
- Very subtle computational grid/nodes/coordinates
- AI communicated by precision and systems, not an AI icon

### Avoid
- Fuji
- Miyajima-style torii in water
- cherry blossoms
- robot/brain/circuit clichés
- neon/cyberpunk
- gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards
- fake decorative Japanese text
- invented founding dates

## Palette
- Paper: `#F4F0E7`
- Ink: `#171816`
- Vermilion: `#C92D27`
- Warm gray: `#AAA59B`
- Hairline: `#D8D1C5`

Target roughly 80% paper / 15% ink / 5% vermilion.

## Typography
Strong grotesque/sans-serif for AI GUILD. Quiet Japanese typography for body copy.

Hierarchy:
1. AI GUILD
2. IZUMO
3. IMAGINE / BUILD / SHARE

## Master graphic
One coherent composition across every asset: oversized vermilion sun, extremely reduced Izumo horizon, and a few geometric strokes evoking Izumo Taisha/shimenawa. It must survive reduction to a business card/favicon.

## Claude Design brief
Treat this art kit as authoritative. Ignore the current website's visual implementation and reuse only its content/structure. Prioritize restraint: if an element can be removed without losing meaning, remove it. Make the result distinctly Izumo, contemporary, intelligent, welcoming, and handmade/technical at the same time.
